About SAFECASE

When something difficult happens, it can be hard to know how to explain it, what details to keep, or where to turn.

SAFECASE is a guided first step.

It helps students and staff record their own account, organize details and supporting material, and explore possible next steps at their own pace.

SAFECASE supports documentation and navigation. It does not investigate allegations, determine guilt or innocence, or replace official reporting and support services.
